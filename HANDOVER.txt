Parrot / echo feature work
--------------------------
- Branch feature/parrot-tg9990 adds an in-process local DMR parrot service, based on the classic HBlink3 playback behaviour but without running a second legacy HBLink daemon.
- hblink4/parrot.py records accepted local group-voice DMRD frames and replays the original packet sequence to the originating repeater/hotspot at the configured cadence after a proper voice terminator.
- run.py installs the isolated parrot hooks on HBProtocol before starting the normal core.
- The parrot is configurable through a top-level `parrot` object. Generic sample defaults are disabled, TG9990, 2-second delay, 60-ms packet cadence and 120-second recording cap.
- When enabled, the configured parrot TG is accepted as a local service even when absent from the normal repeater TG allow-list.
- The parrot TG receives an empty normal route cache: it is never fanned out to another local repeater, HomeBrew outbound, or OpenBridge trunk.
- A numerically identical TG received from an external trunk does not invoke the local parrot.
- Local-to-network DMRD translation is respected, so a local RF alias can map to canonical TG9990.
- Playback is cancelled if the originating repeater disconnects or reuses the slot before/during echo.
- Calls that exceed the configured recording cap or end through timeout/fast-terminator handling are discarded instead of replayed incompletely.
- Regression coverage is in tests/test_parrot.py; operator documentation is docs/parrot.md.
- Production enablement will be done through hblink-website policy reconciliation after this HBlink4 branch passes CI and is merged.
